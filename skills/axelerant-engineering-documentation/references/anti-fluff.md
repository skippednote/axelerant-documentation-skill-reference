# Writing review

Each page answers a real question. Trace every claim to source, execution, an incident or a decision. Do not invent missing steps to fill a template.

Delete vague praise, repeated setup, per-function narration and future promises. Explain the rejected alternative and cost, not just what exists. Reference should come from schemas, help output or source-backed tables.

The checker reads the shared fluff-terms.txt. An exemption names one checker and has a reason; it never disables structural or freshness rules.

Before finishing, confirm every link exists, every claimed command ran, and every evidence date says what was actually checked.
